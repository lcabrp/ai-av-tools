# Growth Partner Accelerator: Onboarding and Execution Guide

This document summarizes the steps required to fully integrate into the Growth Partner accelerator program, focusing on minimizing administrative friction and maximizing learning efficiency to facilitate fast client acquisition and revenue generation.

***

### 🔑 Key Topics

#### 1. Initial Incorporation (Getting Started Fast)
The initial focus is completing a set of simple but mandatory onboarding steps to fully activate participation in the program.

*   **Sign Contract:** Immediately sign the official Growth Partner Agreement/Contract.
*   **Schedule Attack Plan Call:** Reserve your dedicated "Plan de Ataque" call with your CED Director. This meeting will:
    *   Show you how to maximize the full potential of the program.
    *   Deepen understanding of Growth Partner requirements.
    *   Facilitate connections with other new participants, building community success.
*   **Complete Incorporation Form:** Fill out the necessary onboarding form to provide maximum information about your background and needs, allowing the team to tailor support.
*   **Connect with Support:** Utilize Fede (the program director) via WhatsApp for any immediate administrative support needed during the incorporation process.

#### 2. Navigating the Central Learning Hub (School Platform)
The "School" platform is designed as a single point of focus, preventing the chaos often associated with multi-platform learning systems (e.g., random WhatsApp groups mixed with videos).

*   **Community Tab:** Your central hub for peer support and announcements:
    *   *General:* Place any questions for quick responses from the team.
    *   *Announcements:* View important updates, client news, etc.
*   **Classroom Focus (Phase 0):** The core content for the Growth Partner Accelerator focuses on structured, actionable processes designed to generate predictable results. This content outlines a path from initial setup to reaching significant revenue milestones ($\$5.7k \rightarrow \$10k/month \rightarrow \$200k+/month$).
*   **Pillars of Learning:** The curriculum is broken down into distinct pillars:
    *   **Pillar 1 (Knowledge):** Focuses on equipping the participant with strategic knowledge. Teaches establishing confidence by mastering **5 Growth Formulas** to be implemented in various digital businesses.
    *   **Pillar 2 (Offer Creation & Client Acquisition):** This is where income generation begins, utilizing the formulas learned in Pillar 1. It provides processes for acquiring clients through both organic and automated means. Includes detailed scripts (e.g., cold calling) designed to be followed regardless of previous experience level (Introvert/Extrovert).
    *   **Pillar 3:** (Structure continues, focusing on deeper implementation.)

#### 3. Continuous Support System (The Calendar / Community)
Support is structured through multiple specialized channels to ensure every challenge—from fundamental questions to advanced deployment—is addressed.

*   **Dedicated Call Types (Calendar):** Specific appointment types are scheduled around participant needs:
    *   *Fundamentals Calls:* Addressing basic and theoretical doubts related to Pillar 1.
    *   *Sales & Acquisition Calls:* Focused on practical strategies derived from implementing Pillar 2 content.
    *   *Funnel & Ads Calls:* Dedicated support for the technical implementation of revenue systems (going beyond just "getting" the client to *keeping* them).
    *   *Roleplay Calls:* Crucial segment allowing participants to practice sales pitches and conversations with peers in a safe, simulated environment.
    *   *Weekly Director Calls:* Direct guidance and overview from Fede, keeping participants aligned with the overarching program goals.
*   **Community Support:** A 24/7 active community is available for general questions and immediate help.

#### 4. Key Mindset Requirements for Success
The speaker emphasizes that while the system provides the best tools, the ultimate success relies on adherence to a specific mindset:

*   **Patience and Focus:** Success requires maintaining consistent focus and diligently following every step provided without shortcutting or becoming discouraged.
*   **Handling Friction:** The journey from the current state to the desired professional level will involve "friction" (difficulty/resistance). This is normal growth; the willingness to challenge oneself leads to forging success (like iron forged by heat).
*   **The Need for Support:** Participants must utilize the available mentorship and community support. Solving large problems requires elevating one's paradigm, which the program facilitates at every step.
